Engineering reading

Method. One-way ANOVA with SSbetween = Σ ni(ȳi − ȳ)², SSwithin = Σ Σ (yij − ȳi)², F = MSbetween / MSwithin, p from the F distribution. Effect sizes η² = SSB/SST and ω². Equal variances: Brown–Forsythe (median-centred Levene) test. Unequal variances: Welch's ANOVA. Non-normal data: Kruskal–Wallis H with tie correction. Pairwise: Tukey–Kramer HSD with the studentized range distribution. All results were checked against SciPy.

The worked examples are illustrative data, not field data from any company. Companion to the article Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) by Semion Gengrinovich.

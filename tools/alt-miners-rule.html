Method notes

Weibull parameters are maximum-likelihood estimates with right censoring. R50 = η(ln 2)1/β; the R-life is tR = η(−ln R)1/β. The Fisher-matrix bound uses the observed information matrix and the delta method on ln tR (one-sided lower limit at confidence C). The chi-square option holds β at its estimate and bounds η with χ²(2r+2) when suspensions are present, χ²(2r) otherwise. The life–stress line is a least-squares fit of ln(life) against the model's stress transform (ln S, 1/kT, or S) through the three levels, separately for the R50 points and for the lower-bound points, so both lines carry the fit through to the application stresses. Damage D = Σ ni/Ni, with Ni read from the selected line at each duty stress. Plotting positions use Johnson's adjusted ranks with Bernard's approximation.
